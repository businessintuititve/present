The Problem

The data are digital.
The workflow is still manual.

Clinical information is distributed across problems, medications, allergies, labs, encounters, documents, and external systems. To answer a single question, clinicians must navigate between these sources and reconstruct the relationships themselves.

Administrative workflows compound the problem through repeated intake, reconciliation, ordering, documentation, and follow-up.

The Solution

What if the chart assembled itself around the question?

CareOS is a FHIR-native overlay that transforms discrete clinical resources into a relational patient view. Instead of requiring users to search through separate sections of the chart, CareOS brings relevant information together around the patient and the clinical task.

How It Works

An overlay — not another EHR.

CareOS sits above existing clinical systems and uses standardized FHIR interfaces to retrieve and organize patient information.

The source EHR remains the system of record. CareOS changes how interoperable information is assembled and presented, not where the clinical record lives.

No rip-and-replace Standards in. Relationships out.

Why FHIR Matters

The interface can travel with the standard.

Traditional workflow redesign is often tied to a particular EHR implementation. CareOS separates the experience layer from the underlying system of record.

Because the application is built around standardized FHIR interfaces, the same relational model can be adapted across FHIR-enabled environments without rebuilding the concept around every proprietary database.

Short term

Reduce navigation and information-reconstruction burden.

Long term

Enable more portable, patient-centered clinical interfaces built on interoperable health data.
